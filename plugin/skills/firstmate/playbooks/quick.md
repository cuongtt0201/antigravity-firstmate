# Playbook: quick

For a lookup that one command or at most three small files can answer: an IP, a command to type, a query, what one function does.

1. Do it yourself: one command or up to three small reads.
2. Answer in at most 10 lines: the answer, then one line of why.
3. Label any claim you did not see in the output as inferred or guess.
4. If it turns out bigger (more reading, a failure to explain, a change), stop and switch playbook (how, investigate or change). Say so in one line.
